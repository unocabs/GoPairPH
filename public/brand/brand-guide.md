# Go Pair PH Social Brand Guide

## Positioning

Go Pair PH is a focused marketplace for people buying and selling running shoes in Central Luzon and NCR. Facebook remains useful for reach; Go Pair PH gives sellers one organized listing they can reuse wherever they sell.

## Voice

- Helpful, concise, trustworthy, and local.
- Sound like one Filipino runner talking to another.
- Use natural English with restrained Filipino humor when appropriate.
- Default to "shoes" or "running shoes." Use "pair" only when it genuinely sounds natural.
- Never invent users, listings, transactions, demand, popularity, urgency, or endorsements.

## Visual System

- Dark navy foundation: `#061826`.
- Primary teal: `#0d9488`.
- Highlight teal: `#2dd4bf`.
- Primary text: `#f8fafc`.
- Secondary text: `#cbd5e1`.
- Premium editorial marketplace composition, not a generic advertisement.
- High contrast, refined spacing, crisp framing, and mobile-readable type.
- Use realistic generic running shoes without recognizable product logos or copied designs.
- Avoid social-platform branding, badges, watermarks, collages, and visual clutter.

## Social Composition System

- Treat daily Facebook graphics as structured explainer posters, not simple product beauty shots.
- Default composition: oversized headline zone at the top, one central explainer element in the middle, grounded running-shoe visual in the lower half, official footer at the bottom.
- The image should explain one practical idea at a glance. A shoe alone under a headline is usually too generic.
- Prefer one of these middle-layer elements:
  - A glowing listing-link card
  - A phone mockup showing a clean listing layout
  - Simple flow or connector shapes suggesting reuse and sharing
  - Minimal channel or repost tiles without platform logos or readable generated text
- Keep the scene premium and sparse. Each element should have a job. Do not fill space just because it exists.
- Headline treatment should feel assertive and editorial. Strong contrast, short phrasing, and a clear emphasized line work better than paragraph-like copy.
- The base image may include neutral device silhouettes, cards, arrows, and layout structure, but generated text must still be avoided. Apply all readable copy deterministically after generation.
- Avoid these failure modes:
  - Isolated shoe on empty background with only top text
  - Generic ecommerce mockup clutter
  - Fake UI text generated by the image model
  - Literal Facebook branding or copied marketplace interfaces

## Official Logo

The canonical raster mark is `public/brand/gopairph-logo-mark-1024.png`.

Never ask an image model to recreate, redraw, spell, or reinterpret the logo. Generate social artwork with a clean reserved footer, then apply the official logo and footer using `scripts/brand-social-image.cjs`.

If the official logo asset is unavailable, do not invent a replacement.
